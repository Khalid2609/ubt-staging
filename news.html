December 2016 · Al Fahal (PDO magazine), p. 12

The Gulf Starch Plant

PDO’s Production Chemistry department spearheaded the launch of a new Omani-owned starch factory on the Sohar Industrial Estate, run by Localine LLC, following a 2013 contract to supply normal and high-temperature drilling starch. The plant was designed with full automation and an on-site QA/QC laboratory, with a technical support partner, English India Clay Limited. The article reported a production capacity of around 8,000 MT per year, double PDO’s annual requirement of 4,000 MT; 21 Omani staff recruited and trained under PDO-sponsored programmes; and an Omanisation rate of about 60% in the first year. The plant was officially inaugurated in the first week of December 2016 with officials from the Ministry of Oil and Gas, the Ministry of Commerce and PDO in attendance.
